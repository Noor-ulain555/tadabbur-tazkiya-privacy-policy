Privacy Policy

Tadabbur & Tazkiya — a calm, offline daily journal

Effective date: 17 September 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile application Tadabbur & Tazkiya (the “App”, package identifier com.technoorstudio.tadabbur) handles information. The App is a private journaling tool for mood, gratitude, intentions and Qur'an reflection. It is built to be offline-first: your journal writing stays on your device and is never uploaded to us or to any server.

By using the App you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the App.

1. Information You Enter (Stored Only on Your Device)

Everything you write in the App is stored locally on your device using standard app storage (shared_preferences). It is not transmitted to us, and we have no ability to access, read, or recover it. This includes:

  • Journal and reflection entries (free writing, gratitude, daily intentions/niyyah, Qur'an reflections, and monthly reflections);
  • Mood / “heart state” selections, themes, and tags you attach to entries;
  • An optional display name you may provide so the App can greet you personally;
  • Your daily-reminder preferences (whether reminders are on, and the time you choose).

Because this data lives only on your device, deleting an entry, clearing all entries from within the App, or uninstalling the App removes it. We keep no copy and offer no cloud backup.

Optional App Lock (PIN & Security Question)

If you enable the optional App Lock, your PIN and your security-question answer are never stored in plain text. The App stores only a salted SHA-256 hash of each, together with a random salt, on your device. These hashes cannot be reversed to recover your PIN or answer and are never transmitted anywhere.

2. Information Collected Automatically by Third-Party Services

While your journal content never leaves your device, the App uses a small number of third-party services for advertising, analytics, and stability. These services may collect certain technical and usage information as described below.

a) Advertising — Google AdMob

The App displays advertisements through Google AdMob (including App Open, interstitial, banner, and native ad formats). To serve ads, AdMob and its partners may collect and process:

  • Your device’s advertising identifier (the App requests the AD_ID permission);
  • Approximate, IP-derived location and device/technical information;
  • Ad interaction data used for delivery, frequency capping, and measurement.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and other regions where it is required, the App uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request your consent before serving personalized ads, and provides a “privacy / ad” options control inside the App so you can review or change your choice. Where consent is not granted or not required, non-personalized ads may still be shown. Learn more in Google’s partner policy and the AdMob privacy information.

b) Analytics — Firebase Analytics (Android)

On Android, the App may use Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the App is used in aggregate — for example, which screens are viewed and which features are used (such as saving an entry, toggling reminders, or enabling the App Lock). These events describe actions and screen names; they do not include the text of your journal entries. Firebase Analytics may process a Firebase installation identifier and device/technical information.

c) Crash & Stability Reporting — Firebase Crashlytics (Android)

On Android, the App may use Google Firebase Crashlytics to report crashes and errors so we can improve stability. Crash reports may include device state, operating-system version, and diagnostic stack traces. They do not include the contents of your journal.

Google’s handling of the data collected through these services is described in the Google Privacy Policy and the Firebase Privacy and Security information.

3. Device Permissions

PermissionWhy the App uses it
INTERNET Required to load advertisements and to send analytics/crash reports through the third-party services described above.
AD_ID Access to the device advertising identifier, used by Google AdMob to serve and measure ads.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS To show your optional daily reminder to journal. Notifications are generated entirely on your device — there is no push server.
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED To re-schedule your local daily reminder after the device restarts, so the reminder keeps working.

The App also reads your device’s time zone locally in order to schedule the daily reminder at the correct local time. This information is used only on the device and is not transmitted to us.

4. How Information Is Used

  • To provide the journaling features and store your entries locally on your device;
  • To show your optional daily reminder;
  • To display advertisements that help support the App;
  • To understand aggregate usage and to diagnose crashes so we can improve the App.

We do not sell your personal information.

5. Data Sharing

We do not receive or share your journal content, because it never leaves your device. The limited technical and usage data described in Section 2 is processed by our third-party providers (Google AdMob, Firebase Analytics, and Firebase Crashlytics) solely to provide their respective services, in accordance with their own privacy policies. We may also disclose information if required to do so by law.

6. Data Retention

Journal content and settings remain on your device until you delete them or uninstall the App. Data processed by the third-party services above is retained according to those providers’ policies.

7. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the App, please contact us so we can address it.

8. Your Choices & Rights

  • Ads consent: where applicable, you can review or change your advertising consent using the privacy/ad options control inside the App.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or limit your advertising identifier in your device’s system settings (Google settings › Ads).
  • Notifications: you can turn the daily reminder off in the App, or disable notifications in your device settings.
  • Your entries: you can delete individual entries or clear all entries from within the App at any time.

Depending on your region, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data. Because your journal data is stored only on your device, you can exercise deletion directly within the App. For requests relating to the third-party services, please contact us using the details below.

9. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information. Your journal content stays on your device, and optional App Lock secrets are stored only as salted hashes. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the “Effective date” above and post the updated policy at the same location. Continued use of the App after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
